legacy lock errors and current documentation. The normal Status sensor reports
an actual ZHA firmware update instead of becoming unavailable when touch polling
is intentionally suspended. No extra radio queries are made during an update.

the dialog against the running HA 2026.10.4 native frontend with simulated device
traffic: desktop/mobile/dark, assignment, learning, finish, Escape and device scope.

unchanged calibration (20/13/6 us; 2/12 ms; median 3; gap 50 ms), ring enabled at
50 percent, and retired endpoints absent. Real OTA to firmware 0.1.62 is ongoing.
